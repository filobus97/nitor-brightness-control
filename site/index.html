Two editions, one app

Same codebase, same settings file. Switch any time.

Nitor

Everything you need to control your monitors' brightness.

  • Brightness at a glanceEvery monitor gets its own tray icon showing its brightness
  • Scroll to adjustHover an icon and scroll — no clicking, no menus
  • Matched screensOne slider for all monitors, calibrated to look equally bright
  • FeatherweightOne small exe, no install, ~0% CPU when idle
  • Private by designNo account, no tracking, no telemetry

Nitor Plus

Nitor, plus automatic brightness and color temperature.

  • Everything in NitorSame tray icons, panel and settings
  • Follows the sunBrightness rises and falls with daylight where you live
  • Reads the roomUses your laptop's light sensor to match the light around you
  • Warm eveningsWarmer colors after sunset for easier late-night viewing
  • You stay in chargeAny manual change pauses automation until the next sunrise or sunset
